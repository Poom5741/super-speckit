# Craft dimensions and vocabulary

Use this reference to structure investigation, not to impose a single aesthetic.

Primary inspiration:

- Vibe Designing Playbook, Design Engineering: https://alibaba-cloud-design.github.io/vibe-designing-playbook/#sec-1.2
- Vibe Designing Playbook, Lexicon: https://alibaba-cloud-design.github.io/vibe-designing-playbook/#sec-appendix

## Dimension guide

### Typography in use

Inspect scale, line length, line height, weight, numeric alignment, truncation, language coverage, fallback behavior, and whether typographic differences correspond to information roles.

### Hierarchy and composition

Inspect first read, task priority, grouping, alignment, contrast of scale, focal competition, relationship between navigation and content, and whether empty space supports comprehension.

### Density and rhythm

Inspect how much information is available per decision, whether repeated spacing communicates structure, whether every section receives equal weight, and whether dense modes preserve scanning.

### Surface and material

Inspect whether backgrounds, borders, shadows, blur, gradients, and depth express containment, elevation, interaction, or brand character rather than decoration alone.

### Images, icons, and data

Inspect semantic accuracy, provenance, crop and focal point, icon consistency, chart choice, axes and units, legends, color dependence, freshness, uncertainty, and truthful comparison.

### Content

Inspect action labels, terminology, tone, ambiguity, error recovery, sensitive-data handling, placeholder realism, localization, and whether generated claims are supported.

### Motion and feedback

Inspect purpose, continuity, duration role, interruption, progress, cancellation, reduced motion, input latency feedback, and whether motion obscures or explains system state.

### Accessibility and responsive craft

Inspect contrast, zoom, reflow, focus order, keyboard access, accessible names, target size, reading order, motion alternatives, and whether task hierarchy survives different viewports and modalities.

### Product-specific expression

Inspect whether the artifact could plausibly belong to an unrelated product, whether brand expression supports trust and task, and whether category conventions are followed or departed from deliberately.

## Writing executable rules

A useful rule contains:

1. the user or product consequence;
2. the observable design behavior;
3. where it applies;
4. an exception or decision rule;
5. the evidence needed to evaluate it.

Do not convert every lexicon term into a rule. Add a rule only when it protects a relevant product outcome or recurring quality decision.
