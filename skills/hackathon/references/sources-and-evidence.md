## Label claims precisely

- **Winner-informed** means the method draws on documented winner experience or research about winners.
- **Project won a prize** requires a named project, event, exact award, and official result or organizer award badge. Identify sponsor awards separately from overall awards.
- **Skill used by a winning team** additionally requires a dated team account or public artifacts tying that skill to the project's discovery process before the result. Cite the connection and label self-reported usage.
- **Method proven to improve winning odds** requires comparative evidence that supports that causal claim. A successful anecdote does not meet this standard.

Keep author claims separate from organizer confirmation. Record contradictory or missing evidence instead of upgrading an uncertain claim. Never describe this packaged skill as prize-proven without a documented usage-and-result chain.
